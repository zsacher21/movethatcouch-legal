Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MOVE THAT COUCH! (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MOVE THAT COUCH! mobile application and related services (“the platform”). By creating an account or using the platform, you agree to this Policy.

MOVE THAT COUCH! is owned and operated by ZM Platforms LLC, a Texas limited liability company.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (only if you choose to enable SMS notifications after account creation)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Additional photos (optional), such as images of vehicles, equipment, storefronts, or other materials you choose to display publicly on your profile
  • Public profile information (optional), including a short bio or description you choose to share with other users
  • Business name or operating name (optional), which may be displayed publicly when interacting with other users
  • Availability or reference addresses (optional), used to display service availability or nearby driver counts
  • Vehicle or service capability information (for service providers), such as whether you operate a truck suitable for furniture delivery or a standard vehicle for small-item or same-location deliveries
  • Stripe account connection details (for users receiving referral bonuses or late cancellation payouts)
  • Service area center points and service radiuses (for service providers), used to determine which service requests you can see

1.2 Service Information

When you create or accept a service, we may collect:

  • Pickup, drop-off, and service addresses and approximate locations
  • Item descriptions, photos, and videos
  • Preferred service dates or time windows
  • Quotes sent and received
  • Booking deposit status and related transaction details
  • Invoice records, invoice payment status, transaction references, and related payment communications associated with provider-generated invoices
  • In-app chat messages between customers and service providers
  • Contact information and communications related to third-party client coordination, booking approval, or payment requests
  • Assembly, mounting, or service-related instructions
  • Photos and videos of walls, rooms, furniture, mounting areas, items, or related workspaces
  • Photos and videos uploaded or recorded by users may include images or audio of private residences, interiors, personal property, voices, or other information related to requested services
  • AI-generated suggestions, request analyses, or automated feature outputs associated with a service request or user profile

1.3 Camera, Microphone & Media Access

MOVE THAT COUCH! may request access to your device’s camera, microphone, or photo and media library when you choose to take photos, record videos, or upload media through the platform.

Camera access is used to allow you to capture photos or videos related to service requests, in-app messages, profiles, or other platform features. Microphone access is used when you choose to record a video with audio. Photo and media library access may be used when you choose to upload existing photos or videos from your device.

These permissions are used only when needed to provide the features you choose to use. MOVE THAT COUCH! does not use your camera or microphone to record you in the background.

1.4 Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type and version (for web views)
  • IP address and general region
  • Basic usage information, such as screens viewed and actions taken

This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the platform. We do not use it for third-party targeted advertising.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Connect customers with service providers and manage service requests
  • Process booking deposits, provider-generated invoice payments, deposit refunds, late cancellation payouts, referral bonuses, and other supported transactions through Stripe
  • Calculate eligibility for promotional programs, referral bonuses, rewards programs, MTC Credits, and other platform incentives
  • Enable and support in-app messaging
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Maintain and improve platform safety, performance, and reliability
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may use aggregated or de-identified data for analytics and platform improvement purposes.

3. AI-Powered Features & Automated Processing

MOVE THAT COUCH! may use AI-powered tools, automated systems, and third-party AI providers to support platform functionality, improve user experience, enhance safety, and assist users in completing or evaluating service requests.

These tools may process information including:

  • Uploaded photos and images
  • Item descriptions and service request details
  • Pickup, drop-off, and scheduling information
  • Provider profile information and public profile content
  • Quotes, request activity, and related service information
  • Messages, notes, or other content voluntarily submitted through the platform

AI-powered features may be used to:

  • Identify or suggest items from uploaded photos
  • Assist users in creating or completing service requests
  • Provide estimated pricing ranges, quote likelihood insights, timing expectations, or request-related guidance
  • Provide profile improvement suggestions
  • Provide quoting guidance, equipment suggestions, or customer communication suggestions
  • Detect potential fraud, abuse, policy violations, or safety concerns
  • Support moderation, quality control, platform operations, and feature improvement

Some AI-powered features may rely on third-party service providers that process information solely on our behalf and for purposes related to platform functionality and operations.

AI-generated outputs, analyses, estimates, predictions, and recommendations may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or incorrect and should not be relied upon as professional, legal, financial, safety, logistics, or transportation advice.

Users remain responsible for reviewing and verifying information before relying on it or submitting it through the platform.

4. In-App Messaging & Contact Details

To protect user privacy, customers and service providers communicate through MOVE THAT COUCH! messaging tools rather than directly sharing contact details.

  • Phone numbers and email addresses are not shared directly between users through the platform.
  • We may review messages or related data when necessary to investigate safety, abuse, or fraud concerns, or to comply with legal obligations.
  • If you update your account information while a service is in progress, the platform may continue using the information originally associated with that service until the service is completed to avoid confusion or disruption.

5. Email, Push & SMS Notifications

MOVE THAT COUCH! uses email, push notifications, and SMS messages to provide transactional communications that support platform functionality. These communications may include account activity, service requests, booking confirmations, service updates, provider status notifications, cancellations, reminders, and customer support messages.

Push notifications and email notifications may be enabled by default and are used solely for transactional purposes. Users can manage their notification preferences for email and push notifications at any time through their account settings within the app.

Push notifications are delivered through device and operating system notification services and do not require a phone number.

SMS notifications are optional and are sent only after a user explicitly opts in by enabling SMS notifications and providing a valid phone number within the app. SMS messages may be used as a primary, fallback, or supplemental notification method when email or push notifications are unavailable or time-sensitive.

MOVE THAT COUCH! does not send marketing, advertising, or promotional SMS messages. Message frequency varies based on user activity.

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, users may reply HELP. Standard message and data rates may apply.

5.1 Third-Party Service Contacts

Users managing services on behalf of a client may provide the client’s phone number so the client can receive transactional notifications related solely to that specific service.

By providing a client’s phone number, the user represents that they have obtained the client’s permission to receive such messages.

MOVE THAT COUCH! does not send marketing or promotional messages to third-party service contacts, and client contact information is used only for service coordination and status updates.

5.2 Third-Party Client Payment Requests

Users managing services on behalf of a client may provide the client’s email address and phone number so the client can receive transactional communications related to booking approval, booking deposit payment, provider-generated invoices, and other service-related payment requests associated with a specific service.

These communications may include booking deposit payment links, provider invoice payment links, booking details, cancellation terms, receipts, payment confirmations, and service-related notifications.

By providing a client’s contact information, the user represents that they have obtained the client’s permission to receive such communications.

MOVE THAT COUCH! does not send marketing or promotional communications to third-party client payment recipients, and client contact information is used only for transactional service-related purposes.

6. Payments

MOVE THAT COUCH! uses Stripe to process booking deposits paid through the platform.

In some cases, service providers may elect to generate invoices through the platform for completed services. When available, customers may choose to pay such invoices using payment methods supported by Stripe.

Stripe may also process deposit refunds, late cancellation fees paid to service providers, referral bonuses paid to users, and payments made through provider-generated invoices.

We may maintain records of booking deposits, refunds, promotional credits, rewards earned, referral bonuses, and related transaction history in order to administer platform features, calculate eligibility for rewards and incentive programs, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

Payment information submitted through Stripe is processed by Stripe in accordance with its privacy and security practices. MOVE THAT COUCH! does not store full credit card numbers or full bank account information on its servers.

7. Location Information

Pickup, drop-off, and other service addresses and approximate locations are used only to support service requests and matching.

Service providers may see approximate locations before a quote is accepted. Exact addresses are shared only after acceptance so the service can be completed.
