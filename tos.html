6.2 Booking Deposits

When a customer accepts a quote, a booking deposit is charged through the platform using Stripe. Booking deposits are calculated using MOVE THAT COUCH!'s booking deposit structure in effect at the time the booking is accepted.

Booking deposit rates, tier structures, maximum booking deposits, and related pricing policies may be modified by MOVE THAT COUCH! from time to time. The applicable booking deposit is the amount displayed to the customer during checkout and accepted at the time of booking.

Depending on the payment method selected by the parties, payment of the remaining balance may occur directly between the customer and service provider or may be facilitated through payment tools made available through the platform, including provider-generated invoices processed by Stripe.

6.3 Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

Customers and service providers may mutually agree to reschedule a booked service. However, for purposes of cancellation and refund eligibility, the original date and time agreed to at booking controls all cancellation logic.

If a customer cancels more than 48 hours before the originally booked service date and time, amounts paid through Stripe may be refunded to the original payment method, and any MTC Credits applied to the booking may be restored to the user's MTC Credit balance in accordance with platform policies.

If a customer cancels within 48 hours of the originally booked service date and time, the service provider receives 100% of the booking deposit.

If a service provider cancels a booked service for any reason, the booking deposit is automatically refunded to the customer.

Important timing rule: The 48-hour window is calculated from the accepted quote at booking, regardless of later discussions or rescheduling.

Stripe processes booking deposit refunds and late cancellation payouts.
